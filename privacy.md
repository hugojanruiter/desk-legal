# Privacy policy

*Last updated: 15 October 2026*

desk is a private, self-hosted personal finance tool. It has one user: its owner, who uses it for their own household accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

**What it reads.** Through Enable Banking, desk reads the balances and transactions of the bank accounts its owner has linked. It cannot make payments.

**Why.** Only to keep the owner's own budget and cash-flow forecast up to date.

**Where the data is kept.** In the owner's private database, on infrastructure the owner controls and only the owner can access. The data is not sold, shared or passed on to anyone.

**How long.** Until the owner deletes it. Access to the bank accounts ends when the consent expires (after at most 180 days) or when the owner revokes it earlier at the bank or at Enable Banking.

**Contact.** The owner of this page: [github.com/hugojanruiter](https://github.com/hugojanruiter).
